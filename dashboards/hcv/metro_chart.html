Universal Voucher Gap Ratio by Renter Households
Across U.S. Metropolitan Areas
The number of income-eligible renter households at a given AMI threshold (30%, 50%, or 80%) cumulatively is collected from CHAS 2022 data. The total number of housing vouchers available per geography — collected from the Picture of Subsidized Housing data — is divided by the total income-eligible renter count to determine the share of need currently being met. This value is then subtracted from 1 to obtain the proportion of eligible renters whose housing needs are not covered by vouchers, which represents the voucher gap ratio.
