How to Use

  • Select Renter Households, Households Under Poverty Level, or Households of Color as the gap weight. Renter Households uses AMI thresholds; the others use poverty status or racial composition.
  • For Renter or Households of Color mode, use the AMI Threshold buttons to switch between <30%, <50%, and <80% AMI.
  • Choose a Geography: State, County, or Tract. Tract view requires selecting a state from the dropdown or clicking the map.
  • Counties or tracts with suppressed or non-applicable data are shown in grey with a status label in the sidebar — no gap values are displayed for these.
  • Toggle PHA Boundaries to overlay Public Housing Authority service areas.
